What's Included

Everything Your Boiler Service Covers

Every annual boiler service carried out by a Gas Safe registered CHP Home engineer in Manchester includes:

✓

Gas tightness test

Checks for gas leaks throughout the system — a legal safety requirement for all gas appliances.

✓

Internal part check & clean-up

Inspection and cleaning of key internal components to maintain efficiency and extend boiler life.

✓

Flue terminal inspection

Ensures the flue is correctly fitted, unobstructed, and safely expelling combustion gases.

✓

Boiler operations & controls check-up

Tests thermostat, programmer, and all controls to confirm everything is responding correctly.

✓

Radiator heat check

Confirms all radiators are heating evenly and identifies any cold spots or system pressure issues.

Manchester Coverage

All Manchester Areas Covered

Manchester is the UK's sixth-largest city and a major hub for heating engineers. From the towering apartments of Deansgate to the Victorian terraces of Rusholme, we've serviced boilers across every type of property in Greater Manchester. Our Gas Safe engineers know the city's housing stock intimately — whether you're in a converted mill building in Ancoats, a 1930s semi in Didsbury, or a modern new-build in Salford Quays. We understand the common issues that affect Manchester boilers: hard water scaling in the southern suburbs, condensate pipe freezes during our infamous winters, and the wear-and-tear from high-usage heating seasons. That's why our annual boiler service is designed to catch these problems early, keeping your warranty valid and your energy bills low. We cover every M postcode without surcharge.
