1. About CHP Home

These Terms and Conditions govern the relationship between CHP Home ("we", "us", "our") and customers ("you") who book services through our website at www.chphome.co.uk.

CHP Home is a trading name of Chungs Heating And Plumbing Limited, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 15554943, VAT registration number 465816168), whose registered office is at 3 Randalls Drive, Crewe, England, CW1 4GL.

CHP Home is a Gas Safe registered business providing boiler services, including a gas safety check on your boiler. A Boiler Service is not a Landlord Gas Safety Record (CP12). By completing a booking through our website, you agree to these Terms and Conditions in full.

2. Our services

We currently offer the following services:

  • Boiler Service — includes gas tightness test, internal part check & clean-up, flue terminal inspection, boiler operations & controls check-up, and radiator heat check. See section 4 (Pricing and payment) for what you receive on completion, which depends on the outcome of the check.
  • Unvented Cylinder Service — standalone or as an add-on to the Boiler Service.
  • Central Heating Inhibitor Refill — standalone or as an add-on to the Boiler Service.

All services are carried out by Gas Safe registered engineers.

The engineers who attend are either self-employed engineers or engineers from subcontractor companies working with CHP Home. They are not employees or workers of CHP Home.

3. Booking process

Bookings are made through the online booking page on our website. A booking is confirmed only after successful payment via Stripe. As soon as payment is successful, our system automatically assigns an engineer and confirms your booking — there is no manual approval step. This assignment matches your chosen date and time slot to an engineer who has made that slot available. Every engineer is Gas Safe registered and holds the appropriate qualifications for the service, so which engineer is assigned does not change the service specification or the standard of work you are entitled to receive.

You will receive an immediate booking confirmation email with your reference number, service details, and chosen date and time slot. Our booking emails do not include the engineer's personal contact details. When your engineer sets off, you will receive a text message and an email, usually 1–2 hours before arrival. The engineer may call you on the mobile number you gave us. They will show you their Gas Safe ID card when they arrive, and their name and Gas Safe details will appear on your Gas Service Record or Gas Warning Notice, as Gas Safe rules require. Rescheduling and cancellation are handled by CHP Home as set out in sections 5 and 6 — including self-service rescheduling online at www.chphome.co.uk/bookings/ before the cut-off described there. If you need to change the time on the day, you can ask the engineer; if they cannot agree a new time, please email us at hello@chphome.co.uk and we will confirm any new date.

We reserve the right to decline a booking if the service address falls outside our covered postcode areas, or if we are unable to fulfil the requested date. In such cases, a full refund will be issued promptly.

4. Pricing and payment

All prices are displayed inclusive of VAT and shown to you on the booking page before you pay. The price shown to you on the booking page and confirmed before you pay — not any other or older figure, such as one in an advertisement or image — is the price that applies to your booking.

Prices are fixed once your booking is confirmed. Payment is taken in full at the point of booking via Stripe. You can pay by card or by the other methods shown on our secure Stripe payment page. All charges for the services you choose, including any add-on services and any surcharge, are listed on the booking page before you pay and are included in your total price. No call-out fees or hidden charges apply. A cancellation fee applies only in the limited case described in section 5.

Your contract for the service you book is with CHP Home, and our obligations for that service are set out in section 8. We remain responsible to you for that service whichever engineer attends. The separate arrangements described below are not part of that contract.

The price you pay covers the service you booked, together with any add-ons selected at checkout. If the engineer identifies a fault or any other work falling outside that service — for example ordering a replacement part, or carrying out additional repair work — any such work would be a new, separate contract between you and the engineer's business, and only if you accept the engineer's quote. It would be entirely outside your booking with CHP Home.

If the engineer offers you a quote for such work and you accept it, the engineer's business — not CHP Home — will give you its own contact details for that separate work, the terms of that contract (the work involved, any parts needed, and the price), and a receipt once you have paid. The quote and receipt will be in the name of the engineer's business and will say that the work is not connected with CHP Home. The engineer's business must also tell you about your own 14-day right to cancel that separate contract.

CHP Home does not quote for, take payment for or guarantee this work. CHP Home is not a party to this separate contract and is not responsible for it — including its price, quality, or completion — and any issue with it should be raised directly with the engineer's business under that contract. You are free to decline any further work; declining does not affect the service you have already booked and paid for through us.

Declining additional repair work does not change our safety obligations. If the engineer finds that your appliance or installation is unsafe, they will explain the situation and, under the industry-wide Gas Industry Unsafe Situations Procedure, ask your permission to turn the appliance off and attach a warning label. A Gas Warning Notice will then be issued recording what was found. Where a situation is immediately dangerous and permission is refused, the engineer is required to report it to the relevant gas emergency service or supplier. Making an appliance safe in this way is a safety requirement rather than chargeable work, but it may mean the appliance cannot be used until the fault is repaired.

What happens after your service

  • If your boiler passes — you will be issued with a Gas Service Record on completion, confirming the checks carried out.
  • If your boiler does not pass — a Gas Warning Notice will be issued, in line with the industry-wide Gas Industry Unsafe Situations Procedure. This is the official confirmation of the issue found.
  • If your boiler needs repairing — the engineer will explain what they have found and may offer you a separate quote for the repair. As set out above, any such work is a separate contract directly between you and the engineer's business, entirely voluntary, and does not affect the service you have already booked and paid for through CHP Home.
  • If your boiler cannot be safely tested or powered on at all — the engineer will let us know and will issue a record stating that the boiler could not be tested and why, and we will contact you as soon as possible to see how we can help. This does not affect your legal rights.

We email your Gas Service Record, Gas Warning Notice or other record (or, for an Unvented Cylinder Service, your Unvented Hot Water Cylinder Record) to the email address you gave when you booked, when your service is completed.

Promotional pricing is valid only within the stated promotional period. Standard pricing applies outside this period.

A VAT invoice or receipt is available on request. If one is required, please contact us at hello@chphome.co.uk following completion of your service, and it will be provided to you by email.

5. Cancellation policy

Your right to cancel (14-day cooling-off period)

Because you book with us online, you have a statutory right under the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013 to cancel your booking within 14 days after the day your booking is confirmed (when your payment succeeds) and receive a full refund — even if your service date is further away than 14 days. You can cancel for any reason. We never charge a cancellation fee during this period, whenever your service date is. The 14 days are counted from the day your booking is confirmed; rescheduling does not extend or restart this period.

We would always rather help you rearrange your booking if that suits you better, so we will first try to offer you a new date, but the choice is yours.

How to cancel. To cancel, please email hello@chphome.co.uk with your booking reference, so that we have a written record. If you tell us in another way, we will help, and may ask you to confirm by email. Your cancellation is treated as made when you first clearly tell us. When you select Cancel on your booking page, you will see a ready-made email that you can send to us, but you do not have to use it.

Your refund. We will refund you in full, without undue delay and in any event within 14 days of the day we receive your cancellation, using the same payment method you used to pay. This does not include the time your bank or card provider takes to credit your account.

If you ask us to carry out the service within that 14-day period and the service is completed before the period ends, your right to cancel ends once the service has been performed. When you book, you tick a box to confirm that you understand your 14-day right to cancel. If your chosen service date (or a new date you choose later if you reschedule) falls within that 14-day period, you also confirm that you expressly ask us to carry out the service on that date and that you will lose your right to cancel once the service has been fully carried out.

If you cancel after asking us to start, the law allows us to charge for the part of the service already provided, in proportion to the full price of the booking; we will tell you the amount before we deduct anything.

After the 14-day period

You can cancel your booking at any time before the day of your service, free of charge and for a full refund. We would always rather help you rearrange than cancel, so we will first try to offer you a new date, but the choice is yours.

If you cancel on or after 00:00 (UK time) on the day of your service — including after your engineer has set off or arrived — we keep a cancellation fee of £10 for a Boiler Service booking, whatever the total price of the booking, to cover our payment-processing and administration costs. We refund the rest.

At present a cancellation fee applies only to Boiler Service bookings; there is no cancellation fee for any other service. If we introduce or change a cancellation fee for any service, we will update these Terms and show the fee on the booking page before you pay; the Terms in force when you book apply to your booking. The fee never applies during your 14-day cancellation period above.

How to cancel. After the 14-day period, to cancel you must email hello@chphome.co.uk so that we have a written record of the time and your instructions. If you contact us in another way, we will help, but we will ask you to confirm by email.

When your cancellation counts. Your cancellation counts from the time we receive your email, as recorded by our email system: if we receive it before 00:00 on the day of your service, no fee applies, even if we reply later.

Your refund. We refund within 14 days of the day we receive your cancellation, to the payment method you used to pay, not counting the time your bank or card provider takes to credit your account. There is currently no self-service cancellation option on the website.

If we cannot access the property

If the engineer attends within your time slot and cannot get in, they will try to call you at least twice and wait at least 30 minutes. If they still cannot get in, or no adult is present, we will contact you to help you choose a new date. If you ask to cancel instead, the £10 cancellation fee for a Boiler Service applies as described above (the fee never applies during your 14-day cancellation period, which is not affected). Please tell us as early as possible if your plans change so that we can offer the slot to another customer.

Cancellations by CHP Home

In the rare event that we need to cancel your booking (e.g. engineer unavailability, adverse weather), we will contact you as soon as possible and offer you a new date or a full refund; the choice is yours. We will process any refund within 14 days of confirming it, not counting the time your bank or card provider takes to credit your account.

6. Rescheduling

You may reschedule your booking free of charge, as often as you need. You can do it online: sign in at www.chphome.co.uk/bookings/ with your email address and the code we send you, open your booking and choose a new date and time. Online rescheduling is available until 23:59 (UK time) two full days before your service date; for example, if your service is on Friday, you can reschedule online until 23:59 on Wednesday. After that point, please email hello@chphome.co.uk and we will do our best to arrange a new date for you, subject to engineer availability.

Rescheduling is subject to engineer availability in your area, and is only possible to a date and time slot at the same price as your original booking. If you would like a slot at a different price, please email us and we will see how we can help.

7. Service areas

We only accept bookings for postcode areas we currently cover. Coverage is checked automatically by postcode at the start of the booking process — if your postcode is not covered, you will be told before any payment is taken. Our coverage changes from time to time as we add new areas, so the postcode check on the booking page always reflects our current service areas rather than any list published elsewhere.

  • All engineers are Gas Safe registered and hold the appropriate qualifications for the services they carry out.
  • We will make sure services are carried out with reasonable care and skill and in accordance with applicable regulations.
  • Your engineer will arrive within the time slot you booked. If they are running late, we or the engineer will tell you before the end of the slot. If no engineer can attend, you can choose a new date at no extra cost or a full refund. This does not affect your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015.
  • If you contact us within 14 days of your service because you believe the work was not carried out with reasonable care and skill, we will arrange for an engineer to return and put it right free of charge, aiming to attend within 5 working days of our arranging the return visit. If you contact us after those 14 days, we will look at what happened and decide how to help. This does not affect your statutory rights.
  • We will contact you promptly if we need to reschedule or if any issue arises before or during the service.
  • We will provide a Gas Service Record, a Gas Warning Notice or, if the boiler could not be tested, a record explaining why (or, for an Unvented Cylinder Service, an Unvented Hot Water Cylinder Record), depending on the outcome of your service, as set out in section 4 (Pricing and payment). A Central Heating Inhibitor Refill on its own does not come with a record.
  • CHP Home is contactable by email at hello@chphome.co.uk.

9. Your obligations

  • Ensure the engineer can get into the property at the agreed time, and have keys or entry details ready if someone else will be letting them in.
  • Ensure that, once inside, there is safe and unobstructed access to the boiler and any relevant heating system components.
  • Provide an accurate service address and contact information at the time of booking.
  • Ensure an adult (aged 18 or over) is present at the property for the duration of the visit.
  • Inform us of any known hazards or relevant conditions at the property prior to the engineer's visit.
  • Ensure parking is available as indicated when you booked. If this changes, tell us by email at hello@chphome.co.uk before your appointment.
  • Keep pets in another room for the duration of the visit.
