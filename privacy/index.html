1. Who we are

CHP Home is a trading name of Chungs Heating And Plumbing Limited, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 15554943, VAT registration number 465816168), whose registered office is at 3 Randalls Drive, Crewe, England, CW1 4GL. Chungs Heating And Plumbing Limited is the data controller for personal data collected through this website. We are a Gas Safe registered business providing boiler services, including a gas safety check on your boiler.

To contact us about data protection matters, please email us at hello@chphome.co.uk (subject: Privacy).

2. What data we collect

Data you provide when booking

  • Full name
  • Service address (full address including postcode)
  • Mobile phone number
  • Email address
  • Parking availability at your property
  • Any remarks or notes you choose to include
  • Selected service type, date and time slot

Payment data

Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number or security code; Stripe gives us limited details such as the amount, payment status and card type. Stripe handles all payment card data and is compliant with PCI DSS Level 1. Please see Stripe's Privacy Policy for full details.

Data created when your service is carried out

  • Photographs taken on site
  • Appliance and property information, readings and defects recorded on Gas Service Records, Gas Warning Notices and Unvented Hot Water Cylinder Records
  • Emails and messages between you and us
  • Booking history (rescheduling, cancellation and refunds)
  • Limited payment details from Stripe (amount, payment status and card type)

If you book for someone else (for example, a tenant), please show them this policy. We use their name, contact number and the service address under our legitimate interests in carrying out the service at their home.

Data created when you use our website

  • Sign-in data (email login codes or password sign-in, and session information)

Analytics data (only with your consent)

If you accept analytics — either by clicking "Accept all" on our cookie notice, or by turning Analytics on under "Manage options" and clicking "Confirm choices" — we use Google Analytics 4 to collect information about how you use the site — pages viewed, approximate location (country and city level), device and browser type, and how you move through the booking flow. Google states that in Google Analytics 4 IP addresses are not logged or stored; they are used only momentarily to derive approximate location and are then discarded. If you reject analytics, none of this is collected and no Google Analytics code is loaded.

3. How we use your data

  • To fulfil your booking — confirming your appointment, assigning an engineer, sending reminders and post-service communications.
  • To contact you — about your booking, rescheduling requests, or cancellations.
  • To keep records of the services provided under your booking — keeping the Gas Service Records, Gas Warning Notices and Unvented Hot Water Cylinder Records, and the other records of the work carried out, so that we can show what was done, follow it up and deal with any dispute (see sections 4 and 6).
  • To improve our service — using aggregated, non-identifiable booking data to understand demand by area and time slot.
  • To send appointment reminders — an email reminder ahead of your service, and a notification by email and text message when your engineer is on their way.
  • To measure how the website is used — we use Google Analytics to see which pages are visited and where people drop out of the booking flow, so we can improve the site. This happens only if you accept analytics cookies.

We do not send marketing emails or texts without your consent, and we do not sell your data to anyone. If we ever ask for your consent to send marketing, you will be able to withdraw it at any time using the unsubscribe link in any message we send, or by emailing hello@chphome.co.uk.

4. Legal basis for processing

  • Contract performance (Article 6(1)(b) UK GDPR) — processing is necessary to fulfil your booking and provide the service.
  • Legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c) UK GDPR) — keeping accounting, payment and tax records, and passing on information where the law requires it.
  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) UK GDPR) — we rely on this basis for the following, and only where these interests are not overridden by your rights:
    • improving service quality and preventing fraud;
    • using the details of an occupant (for example a tenant) given to us by the person who booked, so that the service can be carried out at their home;
    • keeping the website and booking system secure and working, including loading our typeface; and
    • keeping our Gas Service Records, Gas Warning Notices, Unvented Hot Water Cylinder Records and other work records for 6 years from the date of service, whether or not the property is let. We keep them so we can show the work carried out, support any warranty or follow-up service, and establish, exercise or defend a legal claim or resolve a dispute (6 years matches the usual time limit for bringing a claim, and Gas Safe Register rules require registered businesses to keep work records for at least 6 years). This does not replace a landlord's own duty to keep gas safety records.
  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a) UK GDPR) — website analytics through Google Analytics, which we only run if you accept analytics cookies. You can withdraw your consent at any time by resetting your choice on our Cookie Policy page, which explains how. Withdrawing consent stops further collection but does not make earlier collection unlawful.

5. Who we share data with

We share your data only where necessary:

  • Stripe — payment processor. Stripe processes your payment on our behalf under a Data Processing Agreement. Stripe also uses some payment information for its own purposes, such as fraud prevention and meeting its legal duties, and for that it is responsible under its own privacy policy. If you pay with any digital wallet or other method offered on the Stripe payment page, that provider also handles your payment under its own privacy policy.
  • Amazon Web Services (AWS) — hosting provider for our booking management system.
  • Amazon SES — transactional email delivery (verification and login codes, password reset, booking confirmation, reminder, cancellation and refund emails, and the email with your service record).
  • GitHub (GitHub Pages) — hosting for the public pages of this website. GitHub records technical information such as visitors' IP addresses, browser type and pages visited, for security, under its own privacy statement.
  • Zoho Mail — email hosting for our customer service mailbox (hello@chphome.co.uk). Emails you send to us are stored with Zoho in its EU data centre.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — we and the engineers use WhatsApp to exchange messages and photographs about your booking (for example, if the engineer cannot get into your property). These are used only to handle your booking. Engineers may also back up their phones to a cloud service of their own choice. We cannot confirm where WhatsApp or those backups store data; it may be outside the UK.
  • Our technology provider — the business that builds and runs our booking platform and prepares our weekly statements of completed bookings.
  • Postcodes.io — postcode lookup. When you enter your postcode at the start of the booking, your browser sends it to Postcodes.io, an online postcode lookup service, to check that it is a valid postcode. Postcodes.io also receives your IP address. We cannot confirm where Postcodes.io processes data; it may be outside the UK.
  • Google LLC (email) — copies of booking notifications for our administrators are delivered to a Google email account.
  • Insurers (ours and the attending engineer's), regulators and our professional advisers — where a claim or possible claim arises; when Gas Safe Register or another regulator inspects our work or asks for information; and our accountants and solicitors.
  • The engineers who attend — the engineers who carry out the services are not employees or workers of CHP Home (see section 2 of our Terms and Conditions). Your name, service address, contact number, email address, parking details and any notes you gave us are shared with the assigned engineer, who may also take photographs on site, so they can carry out the service you booked. When your service is completed, we email the record to the email address you gave when you booked. A copy of the record issued to you (a Gas Service Record, Gas Warning Notice or Unvented Hot Water Cylinder Record) is also sent to our administrators and to the engineer who carried out the service. The engineer may keep a copy only where the law or Gas Safe rules require it. It contains your name, service address and details of the work carried out.
  • Notification provider (The SMS Works, UK) — we notify you shortly before your engineer arrives by email (sent through Amazon SES, above) and by text message. For the text message, your mobile number is shared with our messaging provider solely for that purpose.
  • Google LLC (Google Analytics) — website analytics. We share this data with Google only if you have accepted analytics cookies. Google acts as our processor for Google Analytics data. We do not use this data for advertising, and we do not run advertising cookies or tracking pixels on this site.
  • Google LLC (Google Fonts) — our pages load the Inter typeface from Google Fonts. This happens on every page load and is not covered by your cookie choice, because it is a request for a font file rather than a cookie. When your browser makes that request, your IP address is sent to Google's servers so the font can be delivered. Google states that it does not use Google Fonts data for advertising or profiling. See our Cookie Policy for more detail, including how to block these requests if you prefer.

Where a provider handles your data on our behalf, we use it under terms that require it to keep the data secure and use it only to provide its service to us. Some providers listed above (such as GitHub, WhatsApp, Postcodes.io, and Stripe for its own purposes) handle data under their own terms.

As explained above, the engineers who attend are not employees or workers of CHP Home. For your booking, the engineer (or the subcontractor company they work for) handles your information only on our instructions. They must keep it confidential and secure, use it only for your booking, and delete it when it is no longer needed, except for a copy of your record (a Gas Service Record, Gas Warning Notice or Unvented Hot Water Cylinder Record), which the engineer may keep only where the law or Gas Safe rules require it. If you agree separately with an engineer to carry out additional work, as described in section 4 of our Terms and Conditions, the engineer's business is responsible for the information collected for that work. If you have any concern about how your information has been handled by an attending engineer, please contact us at hello@chphome.co.uk and we will look into it.

International transfers

Some of our providers (including Stripe, Amazon Web Services, GitHub, Meta (WhatsApp), Google and Postcodes.io) may process data on servers outside the UK, including in the United States. We cannot always confirm where each provider stores data. Zoho Mail stores data in the European Union.

We will disclose your data to law enforcement, regulators or the gas emergency service where the law or gas safety procedures require it.

6. How long we keep data

  • Booking details on our platform — your name, service address, phone number, email address and notes are kept for at least 2 years from the date of service, and after that until you ask us to delete them, except for the details that appear on your service record (Gas Service Record, Gas Warning Notice or Unvented Hot Water Cylinder Record) and in our accounting records, which are kept as described below.
  • Your sign-in account — your email address and, if you use one, your password are kept until you ask us to delete the account.
  • Gas Service Records, Gas Warning Notices, Unvented Hot Water Cylinder Records and other work records — kept for 6 years from the date of service, then deleted, unless we need them for a claim or complaint that is still open. This applies whether or not the property is let. Our booking system keeps them for about 3 months only; for the rest of the 6 years we keep our own copies.
  • Photographs and messages sent by WhatsApp — kept with the work record for 6 years from the date of service where they form part of it, otherwise for as long as needed.
  • Accounting, payment and tax records — kept for 6 years from the end of the financial year they relate to, as tax law requires.
  • Enquiry messages — kept for as long as reasonably necessary to reply to you. Messages that relate to a booking are kept for the booking retention period instead.
  • Engineers' phones — our contract requires engineers to delete customer photographs from their phones after sending them to us, and to delete call records within 30 days after the 14-day period in which you can ask for a return visit ends (see section 8 of our Terms and Conditions). We cannot guarantee that every engineer does so.
  • Verification and login codes — the one-time codes we email you to verify your email address or sign in to your bookings expire after 10 minutes.
  • Analytics data — visitor-level and event-level data in Google Analytics is retained for 14 months, after which it is automatically deleted. Aggregated reports, which do not identify individuals, may be kept for longer. The analytics cookies themselves last up to 2 years, or until you delete them.
  • Cookie choice — the record of whether you accepted or rejected analytics is stored in your browser for 180 days, after which we ask you again.

7. Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure — ask us to delete your data where there is no longer a lawful reason to hold it.
  • Right to restriction — ask us to stop actively processing your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to withdraw consent — where we rely on your consent, such as website analytics, you can withdraw it at any time by resetting your choice on our Cookie Policy page. The cookie notice will then reappear the next time you load a page, where you can choose "Reject all".

We do not make automated decisions about you that produce legal effects or similarly significant effects, and we do not build marketing or advertising profiles about you. Our booking system does assign an engineer automatically once your payment succeeds, but that is purely scheduling logic: it matches your chosen date and time slot to an engineer who has made that slot available. Every engineer is Gas Safe registered and holds the appropriate qualifications for the service, so which engineer is assigned does not change the service you receive, the price you pay, or any right you have under your booking.

Providing your name, service address, contact details and payment information is necessary for us to enter into and carry out your service booking; if this information is not provided, we will not be able to accept your booking.

To exercise any of these rights, please email hello@chphome.co.uk with the subject line "Data Rights Request". We will respond within one calendar month (or up to two further months for complex requests, in which case we will tell you why). Some of these rights are limited where we must keep records, for example for the reasons in section 4.

You can complain to us about how we use your data by emailing hello@chphome.co.uk. We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days, look into it without undue delay, keep you updated and tell you the outcome. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commission (formerly the ICO) at ico.org.uk.

8. Cookies

We use a small number of cookies and browser storage items on this site: those needed to make it work, one item that remembers if you closed our promotional banner, and Google Analytics cookies that are set only if you accept them. Our pages also load fonts from Google; this is a request for a font file, not a cookie. For the full list, how long each one lasts and how to change your choice, please see our Cookie Policy.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will also tell customers with an upcoming booking by email.
